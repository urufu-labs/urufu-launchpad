## Why a submodule (not vendored source)

Matches the pattern already used in `contracts/lib/` (forge-std, openzeppelin,
solady, v4-core, v4-periphery). Bumping upstream is one `git submodule update
--remote`, drift from upstream is visible in `git status`, and reviewers can
diff our pin against any newer tag with a single command.
